Privacy Policy

Effective 22 August 2026 · Balloon Crumbs is operated by Holt Racing Limited (“we”, “us”).

In short: Balloon Crumbs is account-free. During an operation it shares the positions and coordination information needed by the balloon and chase crew. It has no advertising, behavioural analytics or data sales. Relay data is encrypted at rest and automatically expires.

Who we are

Holt Racing Limited is registered in England and Wales (company number 12633258), with registered office at Hockford Cottage, Ash Road, Pirbright, Woking, Surrey, GU24 0DE. It is the controller for the personal data described here. Privacy enquiries can be sent to privacy@tailendcharlie.app.

Information used by the app

  • Precise location and flight data: latitude, longitude, time, accuracy, altitude, vertical speed and, when available, movement information. These provide the live balloon and chase map, trails, rendezvous and route guidance.
  • Operation information: an installation identifier, display name, role, balloon or vehicle assignment, operation status, landing-zone and route changes, alerts and short crew messages.
  • Optional contact information: a rider or crew phone number, or an emergency contact name, number and medical note, only when the user deliberately shares it with permitted operation roles.
  • Notifications: a device push token may be registered so the relay can deliver operation notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Preferences, downloaded map material, local tracks, completed-flight records and diagnostic logs remain on the device unless a feature clearly says that it will share them. The app does not use advertising identifiers.

Who can receive it

People with the private operation code can receive the operation data needed for their role. A user may also create a revocable, time-limited, read-only safety link for a trusted contact. Treat operation codes and safety links as private.

We use service providers only to run requested features: our hosted relay and Cloudflare for delivery, Firebase for Android push notifications, Open-Meteo for forecast wind, OpenAIP for aeronautical context, and mapping, geocoding or road-routing providers for maps and routes. Those providers may process network information such as an IP address when handling a request. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Retention and security

Relay traffic is encrypted in transit and operation event bodies are encrypted at rest. Live location events expire from the relay after no more than 30 minutes; optional phone and emergency-contact shares after no more than two hours; and other operation data after no more than 72 hours. Ending an operation shortens the remaining retention period. Short planner codes and their encrypted plans expire after 30 days. Very short-lived pre-start presence is replaced or expires within minutes.

Local records remain on the device until the user removes them or removes the app. No security measure is perfect; crews should stop sharing and end the operation as soon as coordination is complete.

Your choices and rights

Location and nearby-device permissions are optional at operating-system level, although live coordination cannot work fully without them. Leaving or ending an operation stops new relay publication. Users can remove local flight records in the app and revoke any safety link they created.

UK data-protection rights may include access, correction, erasure, restriction and objection. Because Balloon Crumbs has no accounts, we may need an operation or link identifier to locate relay data and must avoid disclosing another participant’s information. Contact privacy@tailendcharlie.app. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Children and changes

Balloon Crumbs is intended for adult balloon pilots and chase crews and is not directed at children. We may update this policy as the alpha product changes; the effective date above identifies the current version.
